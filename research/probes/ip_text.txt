The current works on autonomous painting systems are often limited to using

style to the final agent.

Appendix E Inference Time Analysis
While the ability to closely mimic the human artistic creation process
determines the practical merits of a stroke based rendering approach
huang2019learning; zou2021stylized; liu2021paint over pixel-based image
generation methods, the time taken to generate the same can help us better
understand the practical usability of a method. In this section, we provide an
analysis comparing the inference time required for our method with previous
works huang2019learning; zou2021stylized; singh2021combining; liu2021paint.
In order to facilitate a better understanding for inference-time requirements of
our approach we define two different variations of our method.
Ours (with StrokeReg). This indicates the default Intelli-paint pipeline
consisting of a Sequential Planner for human-like painting sequence
initialization and Stroke Regularizer for predicting the most efficient
brushstroke decomposition for each test image 𝐼.
Ours (w/o StrokeReg). We note that since the stroke regularization process is

considerably slower than the Sequential Planner agent. Also, since performing

we define a variant of Intelli-paint which only relies on sequential predictions
